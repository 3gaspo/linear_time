# Results recap

No TIME benchmark experiment has been run or analyzed yet. Synthetic checks
verify numerical fitting and reporting behavior; they provide no evidence
about forecasting performance on the TIME datasets.

The first evidence needed is the seedless default joint-panel smoke/full
experiment over the nine-cell L-H grids. It must establish semantic-panel and
history feasibility, validation-selected alpha, metric availability, exact
primal/dual state sizes and CPU timings. The seeded seen/unseen user study and
the three variate-sharing modes follow as separate experiments.
